# Brewing guide

These are the recipes we use in the shop. Weigh the coffee and the water: spoons and cups vary too much.

## V60

Use 15 grams of coffee, ground medium fine, for 250 ml of water at 93 degrees. Pour 40 ml first and wait 30 seconds, then pour the rest in slow circles. The whole brew takes about two and a half minutes.

## French press

Use 30 grams of coarsely ground coffee for 500 ml of water just off the boil. Stir once, put the lid on and wait four minutes before pressing slowly.

## Turkish coffee

Use one heaped teaspoon (7 grams) of extra fine coffee per cup, with cold water and sugar to taste. Heat slowly and take the pot off the heat just before it boils, when the foam rises.
