# Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders placed later, or at weekends, leave on the next working day.

## Carriers

Parcels travel with Yurtiçi Kargo. The tracking code arrives by email and SMS as soon as the parcel is handed over. Deliveries to Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir usually take one working day; the rest of Turkey takes two to three.

## Costs

Shipping is free for orders above 750 TL. Below that it costs 49.90 TL, whatever the size of the parcel. Express delivery the next morning is available in Samsun for 89.90 TL.

## Changing the address

The address can be changed until the parcel leaves the warehouse. After that, the customer asks Yurtiçi Kargo directly with the tracking code, or picks the parcel up from the nearest branch.

## Customs

We only ship within Turkey for now. Orders from abroad are refunded in full.
