# Support team handbook

How the Demlik support team works. Read this before your first shift.

## Response times

We answer every email within 4 working hours and every phone call on the same day. Messages about damaged or missing parcels come first.

## Escalation

Anything about a payment taken twice, a lost parcel worth more than 2000 TL, or an angry customer who asks for a manager goes to the team lead, Elif Şahin. Outside working hours, write in the #support-urgent channel.

## Tone

Write the way you would speak to a regular at the counter: friendly, plain and short. Never blame the carrier in front of the customer; say what we will do next.

## Goodwill gestures

Support can give a 10 percent discount code, or free shipping on the next order, without asking anyone. Anything larger needs the team lead.
