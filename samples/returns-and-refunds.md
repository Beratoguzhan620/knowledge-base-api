# Returns and refunds

Demlik customers can send back any product within 14 days of delivery. Coffee and tea can be returned only while the bag is still sealed; opened bags are refunded only when the product is faulty.

## How to start a return

The customer opens the order in their account and chooses **Return an item**. They get a prepaid Yurtiçi Kargo code by email, valid for 7 days. A return without a code is accepted at the warehouse, but the customer pays the postage.

## Refunds

Refunds are paid back to the original card within 3 working days of the parcel reaching our warehouse in Samsun. Bank transfers for orders paid at the door take up to 5 working days. Shipping costs are refunded only when the whole order comes back.

## Exchanges

A grinder or kettle can be exchanged for another model once per order, free of charge. The price difference is charged or refunded to the same card. Coffee is not exchanged; it is returned and ordered again.

## Faulty or damaged products

If a product arrives damaged, the customer sends a photo within 48 hours. We send a replacement straight away, without waiting for the damaged item to come back. Faulty equipment is repaired or replaced within the two-year warranty.
